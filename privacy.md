# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 15, 2026**

DEYEKA Development ("we", "us", or "our") operates Discord bots and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Discord bot.

## 1. Information We Collect

Our bot may process limited information necessary to provide its features, including:

* Discord User ID
* Discord Username and Display Name
* Discord Server/Guild ID
* Discord Channel ID
* Discord Role ID
* Messages or commands sent to the bot when required for a specific feature
* Information related to bot commands, moderation actions, or server configuration

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment information, or private messages unrelated to the bot's functions.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information processed by the bot may be used to:

* Provide and operate bot features
* Respond to commands and interactions
* Perform moderation and server-management functions
* Maintain server-specific configurations
* Prevent spam, abuse, or misuse of the bot
* Troubleshoot errors and improve the service

## 3. Data Storage

Some information may be temporarily processed or stored by the bot depending on the features enabled on a Discord server.

We only retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality or maintain the service.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user information.

Information may only be processed or shared when reasonably necessary to operate the bot, comply with applicable law, protect the service, or prevent abuse.

## 5. Discord

Our bot operates through Discord and uses information made available through the Discord platform and its APIs.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 6. Data Deletion

If you believe that information associated with you has been stored by our bot and you would like it deleted, you may contact the DEYEKA Development team.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant Discord account or server configuration.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws in their country.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by our services. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact the DEYEKA Development team through our official Discord server or available support channels.
